LIFE CITY: YAOUNDÉ — asset notes

Procedural surface textures (plaster, concrete, asphalt, sidewalk, earth, grass, metal, wood, roof)
are generated at runtime in src/render/surfaces.ts. They are original to this project.

No third-party building or vehicle GLB is bundled yet.
Optional files may be added later under:
  characters/ animations/ buildings/ roads/ vehicles/
  furniture/ vegetation/ textures/ audio/ ui/

Only add GLB/GLTF files whose license allows use in this game, and append the source and license below.

src/assets/loadModel.ts returns null when a file is missing. The game keeps its procedural fallback.
